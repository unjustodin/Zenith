Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-11-04
  Features:
    - Stacked belt items (stack inserters' stacks) are sent with their size, for the 3D client to draw them as piles.
    - Alt-mode icons travel with each building, on every loaded machine (no 400-machine limit); requester and buffer chests show their first four requests (issue #9).
    - Alt-mode's inserter, pump and machine arrows: the fluid connections a recipe uses and the recycler's output (issue #9).
    - The client chooses how many belt items move (Belt updates, or Unlimited) and how far; the bridge's own 6,000 cap and the entity distance no longer limit it.
  Optimizations:
    - Belt lanes that didn't change aren't read or sent again: the bridge's belt cost at a busy base fell from 12-15 ms to about 3-5 ms a tick.
    - Alt-mode costs the bridge the same as when it is off.
  Bugfixes:
    - Right-click mines trains, wagons and cars with their fuel and cargo, not the rail under them (issue #10).
    - Full belts that flow were sometimes held still for seconds and then jumped.
    - Belt lines that moved on by whole slots are read along their length, splitters and undergrounds included, so belts no longer show an item short or double.
    - The edge of the moving-items area no longer goes back and forth while standing still.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-27
  Features:
    - Streams the world near the 3D client's camera over localhost UDP: chunks with their tiles and entities, changes as they happen, moving things, items on belts, on the ground and in inserter hands, fluids, power, day and night.
    - Plays a character for the client: walking, mining, building (with fast-replace, rails, quality and ghosts), crafting, inventory, quickbar, shooting, driving, picking up.
    - Machine screens: inventories, recipes, filters, limits, combinators, circuit and logistic conditions, turret priority targets, train schedules and stops, logistic chests and roboports.
    - Blueprints, books, deconstruction and upgrade planners, copy and paste, wire tools.
    - Research, map and map tags, production and kill statistics, chat, Factoriopedia data, the armour's equipment grid.
    - Space Age: space platforms, the other planets (demolishers, lightning, Gleba's plants and spoiling, Aquilo's freezing), cargo pods.
    - Factorio's alerts worked out for a character without a player.
    - Several 3D clients on one server, each with its own character.
    - Single-player (hybrid) mode plays the player's own character; on a server it can stand in for a player while they're away.
    - Console commands /f3d-status, /f3d-selftest, /f3d-platform, /f3d-player, /f3d-profile.
